# US09 - Seller views revenue statistics

**Issue:** #21  
**Priority:** P1 - Should have  
**Story Points:** 5  
**Related route:** `/seller/analytics`

## 1. User Story

As a seller, I want to view revenue generated from my completed orders, so that I can understand the performance of my store.

---


## 2. Eligible Orders

Revenue is calculated only from orders whose business status represents successful completion.

The canonical eligible status for US09 is:

`COMPLETED`

Orders with states such as:

- pending;
- processing;
- cancelled;
- rejected;
- refunded;

must not contribute to completed revenue.

US08 should use a compatible order-status model so that `COMPLETED` has an unambiguous meaning across the system.
---


## 3. Revenue Calculation Rule

Revenue must be calculated from the seller's own order items rather than from the total value of the entire order.

For seller `s`:

`Revenue(s) = Σ(unitPriceAtPurchase × quantity)`

for all order items satisfying:

- `orderItem.sellerId = s`;
- `order.status = COMPLETED`;
- the order completion time falls inside the selected date range, when a range is applied.

### Example

An order contains:

Seller A:

- Mouse: `2 × $20 = $40`

Seller B:

- Keyboard: `1 × $50 = $50`

Order total:

`$90`

Revenue shown to Seller A must be:

`$40`

not `$90`.

---


## 4. Historical Price

Revenue must use the product price recorded at the time the order was placed.

Conceptually:

`orderItem.unitPriceAtPurchase`

must be used instead of:

`product.currentPrice`

This prevents historical revenue from changing if a seller changes a product's current price later.

---


## 5. Seller Data Isolation

### BR7 - Seller revenue isolation and calculation

Seller analytics must include only order items belonging to the authenticated seller and coming from completed orders.

A seller must never see revenue generated by another seller's products.

For every analytics request:

`sellerId = authenticatedUser.id`

The seller identity must not be accepted as an arbitrary client-supplied identifier.

---


## 6. Date Range Filtering

The seller may optionally select a date range.

Revenue within a date range includes only eligible order items whose order reached `COMPLETED` status within that range.

The relevant timestamp is:

`completedAt`

rather than the original order creation time.

Conceptually:

`from <= completedAt <= to`

If no date range is supplied, analytics displays revenue across all completed orders available to the seller.

The exact timestamp/timezone representation will be defined in the API and architecture milestone.

---


## 7. No-data Behaviour

If no completed orders exist for the selected period:

- total revenue is shown as zero;
- the analytics page remains valid;
- the user receives a clear empty-state message.

Suggested display:

`Total revenue: 0`

`No completed sales found for this period.`

This is not treated as an application error.


---


## 8. Analytics Flow

```text
/seller/analytics
        |
        v
Authenticate seller
        |
        v
Determine authenticated seller ID
        |
        v
Read seller-owned order items
        |
        v
Filter orders by COMPLETED status
        |
        v
Apply completedAt date range
        |
        v
SUM(unitPriceAtPurchase × quantity)
        |
        v
Return seller revenue
```

---

## 9. Multi-seller Order Behaviour

An order may contain products belonging to multiple sellers.

Revenue calculations must therefore operate at order-item level.

The total order value must not automatically be attributed to every seller participating in that order.

Each seller receives analytics only for the subset of order items that belong to them.

---

## 10. Acceptance Criteria Coverage

### AC1

Completed orders contribute to total seller revenue.

Covered by Sections 2 and 3.

### AC2

A selected date range limits analytics to the chosen period.

Covered by Section 6.

### AC3

A period without completed orders returns zero with a clear empty state.

Covered by Section 7.

### AC4

Revenue belonging to other sellers is never included.

Covered by Sections 3, 5 and 9.
